Hannah McCall

Hannah is a fifth-year PhD candidate. She previously earned her BA in physics from Washington University in St. Louis and her MSc in astrophysics from the University of Bonn, where she analyzed eROSITA data of the Virgo Cluster. During her PhD, Hannah has worked on both imaging and spectroscopic analyses of galaxy clusters with a variety of X-ray instruments, focusing on the study of AGN feedback. She has also analyzed observations of the variable supermassive black hole Mrk 766 with the goal of constraining its outflows.
