Update - currently open positions:
- We have an open position for a graduate student.
- Several graduate/rotation projects and undergraduate projects are available.

Interested in joining us?

Prospective undergraduate students
If you are interested in doing research with the group, send Irina an email to ask about possibilities. Please include your CV, transcripts, and a short description of your past research experience (if any) and your research interests. 

Prospective UChicago graduate students
If you are a prospective UChicago graduate student, please apply through the UChicago A&A Department (typically the deadline is in November of each year). If you have any questions or interested in available opportunities, send Irina an email.

UChicago graduate students
If you are a current/incoming UChicago graduate student interested in doing a rotation, send Irina an email, or stop by our group meetings. These meeting are currently on Fridays at 11 am on campus in ERC 517 or 545; all UChicago students are welcome!

Prospective postdocs 
Interested candidates are encouraged to apply for the E. Margaret Burbidge Fellowship and other postdoctoral fellowships available through the department and the KICP. We also host NASA Hubble Fellows.
From time to time, additional postdoctoral positions become available within our research group. Prospective applicants are welcome to email Irina with a CV to inquire about current or upcoming opportunities.

Current graduate students and postdocs at other institutions who are interested in collaborating with or joining the group are also welcome to get in touch with Irina. We encourage prospective collaborators to consider giving a talk at our group meeting or one of the department’s seminars as an opportunity to introduce their research.
